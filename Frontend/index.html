Introduction

Nowadays, when it comes to data protection, it often happens that hackers are one step ahead. And one of the dangerous attacks is called the Zero-Day Attack.

Perhaps, when we talk about hacker attacks, a Zero-Day attack does not immediately come to mind, but it is also dangerous. It is called that way because hackers find a vulnerability in a system or some application that the developers are not even aware of or security update is not available yet. And while the creators do not know about this vulnerability, or trying to make security update, hackers are already exploiting it with might and main.

Anyone can be hit by such an attack. Companies and services could face database leaks and huge losses, while users risk losing passwords or personal information.

In my blog, I will tell about how Zero-Day attacks work, what happens in practice, and most importantly, how to defend yourself in such situations, be it a company that has been hacked or a user who needs to save their personal data.
